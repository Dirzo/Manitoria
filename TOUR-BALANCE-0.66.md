# Manitoria 0.66 — Five-cup difficulty balance

## What the saved run exposed

The Soggy Biscuit Brigade won cups one and two, entered cup three at level 9 and finished fifth. The old Champion schedule raised rivals to level 10, granted a third finished item, introduced wild loadouts and upgraded skills together. Some rivals carried duplicate wild items. Several loadouts still followed the old physical/magical role assumptions rather than the updated AD/AP kits.

A read-only copy of the fallen save was used for replay comparisons. Its campaign, result and items have not been changed. Replays use copied heroes and controlled seeds; they do not reproduce every historical decision or exact pre-match hero state.

## Earned progression

Match XP rises modestly from 1.9x to 2.0x. Cup participants receive 160 training XP when their club qualifies for another cup. This applies to actual cup participants, preserves normal level-up choices, and is shown in the news and scouting information. It does not award gear or reroll skills. Final cups and eliminated clubs do not receive a next-cup training award.

Four wins per cup with normal XP priorities produce roughly these opening levels:

| Cup | Player | Keeper rival floor | Standard rival floor | Champion rival floor |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 6 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
| 3 | 8 | 7 | 8 | 9 |
| 4 | 11 | 9 | 10 | 11 |
| 5 | 13 | 11 | 12 | 13 |

Two fifths of rival identities use a floor one level lower after cup one. Rivals retain XP they actually earn, so a successful club can exceed its floor. These are fixed progression rules; rivals do not adjust to the player's current roster power. Losses, different bracket paths, focused XP and recruitment change the player's actual levels.

## Difficulty and equipment

Keeper starts at 94% rival stat quality and reaches 96.5% before within-cup modifiers. Standard progresses from 100% to 102.5%; Champion from 104% to 108%. Within-cup quality adds at most 2.4 percentage points, replacing the old 7.5-point escalation.

A continuous finished-item budget grows across the tour: 2.6, 2.9 or 3.1 finished-item equivalents by the final stage for Keeper, Standard or Champion. Deterministic bearer thresholds distribute the next finished item across the team; loose components bridge the gaps. Finished items use the champion's current skill and evolution recommendations. Duplicate rival items are removed.

Champion wild effects arrive from cup four, at most one per bearer, on a minority of rivals. AD users receive appropriate attack-oriented wild options; AP users receive spell or survival options. Rare skill upgrades spread across cups and rival identities. Forced Legendary upgrades are limited to a minority of Champion rivals in cup five. Naturally earned upgrades remain possible.

The survival rules remain: Keeper top five, Standard top four, Champion top three. Match rewards, item prices and the roster lock within cups are retained. All 32 champions, current AD/AP scaling, item effects, hex combat and VFX remain included.

## Validation method

The combat sweeps use eight overlapping roster cohorts covering all 32 species, all three difficulties and all five cup openings. Teams receive attainable XP, normal first-choice skill upgrades and staged suggested equipment. Each matchup is run from both sides. Fixed opponent skill choices and temperament/stat rolls can cause variation; these samples compare tuning changes and are not population win-rate estimates or guarantees that every build can beat every opponent.

Structural checks verify natural XP levels, difficulty ordering, staged rarity, legal item slots, valid gear, absence of duplicates, limits on wild effects and compatibility with current suggested builds. Saved-run replays also include its actual four- and five-champion rivals. Detailed final combat results are appended below.

Changes are local and have not been pushed to GitHub. Existing saves remain readable. An already-fallen run is not revived; updated outfitting applies when a new cup is prepared.

## Final measured results

The retained comparison uses 480 old-ramp fights, 240 shipping-ramp fights and 56 saved-roster replays (776 comparison fights total). Five of the 240 shipping sweep fights reached the existing 150-second limit; the normal health-based result resolved them. No simulation hung. Results below are sampled wins from mirrored first-choice builds, not predicted player win rates.

| Difficulty | Cup 1 | Cup 2 | Cup 3 | Cup 4 | Cup 5 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Keeper | 15/16 | 16/16 | 16/16 | 15/16 | 16/16 |
| Standard | 9/16 | 14/16 | 11/16 | 13/16 | 9/16 |
| Champion | 7/16 | 6/16 | 5/16 | 4/16 | 3/16 |

The old Standard sweep declined from 18/32 in cup one to 0/32 in cup five. The old Champion opening, with an extra level and skill on every opponent, was 0/32 in this benchmark; the new opening is 7/16. Expert choices and the stronger real saved roster perform differently from the automatic first-choice builds.

The Soggy Biscuit Brigade copy won **9/28 old-opponent replays and 22/28 revised-opponent replays**, keeping the saved player equipment and skills. Ashen Crown remains a difficult counter-matchup at 0/4 in the revised copy, so the change does not make every matchup an automatic win.

Final structural/save checks: 13,626 progression and gear checks; 26 actual cup-training/save checks; 464 skill/item/recruitment checks; 1,228 item feedback/audio checks. All passed. The full cup fixture verified that training is earned once, unused reserves are excluded, and player upgrade choices remain pending.
