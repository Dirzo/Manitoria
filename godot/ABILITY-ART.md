## Final generation prompt

Create a production fantasy RPG ability icon atlas as ONE square 2048x2048 image containing exactly 8 columns by 8 rows of equally sized square cells. No gutters, no text, no labels, no numbers, no outer border. Each cell is an independent lavish painted game spell icon with a dark midnight blue background, dramatic luminous colored silhouette, crisp readable centered emblem, intricate painterly gold accents, premium fantasy card art. Keep each subject within the middle 80 percent of its cell. Uniform cell alignment is essential for use as a texture atlas.
