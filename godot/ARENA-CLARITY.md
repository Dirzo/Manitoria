# 0.15 Arena Clarity

Removed combat rings, circular windup/radius outlines, expanding cast ripples, guardian rings and shield bubbles. Removed the central floor rings and radial tick decoration. Team identity uses health-bar and name colors. Shields use a slim blue bar above health; numeric shield totals remain available. Hero names and health displays are larger.

Windups retain the animated hero, compact charge effect, skill name/countdown and cast bar. Projectiles, directional spell strikes, rising ground effects, elemental ground wisps and small impact bursts remain visible. Support spells use short rising streaks. Wind and whirlwind effects use short directional strokes instead of complete rings. Damage and healing figures still show actual combat results. Fading text outlines now fade with the text.

Four new short impact samples distinguish flesh, stone, metal and arcane contacts. Physical hits sound on actual damage; special projectile impacts sound on arrival. Basic projectile impact events do not duplicate their damage sounds. Repeated spell damage, regeneration and shield ticks stay silent. Casts are softer, deaths have higher audio priority, and the arena score is 4 dB lower with brief ducking beneath major events. The existing bright arena score is retained.

Validation: no visible torus meshes in an active battle including representative spells; shield display, cast identification, impact routing, audio throttling/pause, all 128 skill timings, fluid animation and tactics regressions tested. Windows exhibition captured and preview encoded with game audio.

Extract the Windows ZIP into a fresh folder and open Manitoria.exe. Keep Manitoria.pck alongside it. Existing saves remain compatible. Title screen version: 0.15.
