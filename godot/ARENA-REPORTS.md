# Manitoria 0.11 — Arena Reports

The post-match report now has animated hero rankings and cumulative team graphs for damage dealt, effective healing, health damage taken, and shield damage blocked. Filter your club, the opposition, or all heroes, then select a hero and switch between Timeline and Abilities. Abilities show resolved uses, damage, healing, and overheal. The continue button stays visible while the report scrolls. Saved match reports use the same interface.

Damage measures actual health removed after mitigation; absorbed shield damage is separate. Healing excludes overheal. Delayed projectiles, damage-over-time and zones retain their original ability attribution. Summoned allies contribute to their owner's totals. Phoenix rebirth is recorded as restoration. Timeline samples are grouped into one-second bins.

Level-up and evolution cards now feature large painted art above shorter descriptions. Hover a description for its full text. The 64-cell art atlas covers all 32 signatures, all discovery mechanics, evolutions and mastery cards; abilities with the same mechanic share its icon.

## Play
Extract the Windows ZIP into a new folder and open Manitoria.exe. Keep Manitoria.pck beside it. The title screen says Windows edition 0.11. Existing campaigns remain compatible. Older reports retain their old totals; detailed ability and timeline data starts with matches played in this version.

For an immediate fight, choose Watch champion exhibition. For progression, continue a campaign or found a club, recruit five heroes, arrange formation and tactics, and enter the arena. Level-up choices remain individual to each hero.
