# Manitoria 0.22 — Card Particles

Combat and the Skill Lab now share particles that follow the actual ability card artwork. The 128 individual paintings and 64 atlas entries supply sampled colors; effect families supply embers, crystals, leaves, feathers, petals, stone shards, stars and wisps. All 384 discovery options and 32 signatures resolve through this system, including skills that share atlas art.

Windups gather energy. Casts release bursts, piercing skills stream along their path, projectiles leave trails, and impacts, healing and new shields produce recipient effects. Existing spell geometry, animations and damage numbers remain. Rare and Legendary casts gain density and bright accents while retaining the artwork's palette.

The simulation clock controls particle movement and fading, including pause, slow motion and battle speed. GPU animation and limits of 1,600 particles / 80 batches bound the added work. Busy native battles still have a broader performance limitation; this release does not claim to resolve it.

No health, damage, targeting, progression, inventory or save format changes. Existing campaigns continue normally. Extract the complete Windows ZIP into a new folder and launch Manitoria.exe beside its matching Manitoria.pck. The menu identifies Windows edition 0.22.

Validation: card profile coverage, rarity density, pause, cleanup, budgets, shield recipients, simulation isolation, arena routing, ring-free clarity and native elemental previews. All 128 individual paintings are retained and verified during packaging.
