# Manitoria 0.24 — Champion Atelier

Kirin is a craftsmanship benchmark, not a shared creature design. All 32 champions retain their own species silhouette, original palette, bones and five existing animation clips. This is an incremental reference-guided detail pass, not a claim that every creature now matches the Kirin sculpt or original paintings perfectly.

The original two 16-creature sheets guide the finishes: shaggy charcoal wolf coats, orange fox fur, pale Yeti hair, lion manes, feathered birds, teal Naga scales, reptile plating, rocky Golem surfaces, living bark, and selected gold ornament only where the design calls for it. Unicorn and Pegasus gain flowing mane/tail strands; the other beasts do not become Kirin variants.

New geometry includes curved coat locks, layered feather and scale details, dorsal accents, shorter and sharper eye surrounds, hooked bird beaks and crests, and fitted ornamental curves. Selected horns receive smoother contours. Fur/hide normal-map intensity is reduced to avoid the previous coarse ridged look. Added details inherit source skin weights and remain attached during combat animations.

The updated GLBs are used in recruitment, roster previews, contestant introductions, battle and Skill Lab. All 32 recruitment portraits are regenerated. Combat abilities, particle styles, damage, campaign progression and saves remain unchanged from 0.23.

Validation: all 32 rigs, normalized weights, bounded geometry, textured materials, five animated clips, finite poses and closed idle/walk loops; Kirin-specific checks; six Godot roster review sheets; native roster and battle smoke checks.

The separate Champion Atelier bundle contains editable packed Blender files, exported GLBs, asset reports, original design sheets and processing scripts. The refinement script expects the previous Manitoria-Blender-Roster bundle beside it as its baseline; the resulting .blend files are independently editable. Fine facial anatomy and bespoke per-species skeletal animation remain further art work.

Extract the entire Windows ZIP into a fresh folder and launch Manitoria.exe beside its matching Manitoria.pck. Check Windows edition 0.24 on the menu. Existing saves continue normally.
