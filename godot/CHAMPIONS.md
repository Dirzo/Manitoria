MANITORIA 0.10 — CHAMPIONS

START HERE
Extract the whole Windows ZIP into a new folder. Open Manitoria.exe and confirm Windows edition 0.10. Keep Manitoria.pck beside the executable. Existing campaign saves still work.

For an immediate demonstration, choose Watch champion exhibition on the title screen. Ten level-10 heroes fight with complete four-ability kits and evolutions. This mode never writes campaign data; Exit exhibition or the report's Back to main menu button returns safely.

For a campaign, found a club, recruit five heroes with the starting gold, prepare their formation and tactics, and enter the arena. Level-ups award individual ability choices; level 10 unlocks an evolution. Continue latest campaign resumes your most recently saved slot.

NEW PRESENTATION
- Animated title-screen hero gallery with idle, attack and cast previews. Models are framed to the available viewport and return to idle after an action.
- A unified club backdrop, short screen fades and clearer roster build summaries.
- Live health, shield and fallen-state panels for both teams. Click a hero portrait to inspect all four ability slots and real cooldowns.
- Brighter arena lighting, a central floor medallion and smaller overhead names.
- Match reports highlight the club MVP and show damage bars, healing, shielding, kills and experience. The continue button remains visible while scrolling.
- Independent music/effects levels in Club > Settings.
- Club-name drafts survive form refreshes and save-slot changes.

CONTROLS
Drag heroes before battle. Use each starter's Tactics button to set orders. In battle, click a side-panel portrait for ability information. Space or Escape pauses; 1/2/4 changes speed. Right-drag orbits the camera; wheel zooms. F11 toggles fullscreen.

VALIDATION

The packaged executable is smoke-tested separately.

The 32 existing Blender creatures and the bright-string/war-drum score carry forward. This release focuses on presentation, battle readability and a complete playable showcase. It does not replace the models with new sculpts.
