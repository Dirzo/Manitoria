MANITORIA 0.25 — CROWNS & EVOLUTIONS

Launch Manitoria.exe from the extracted Windows folder.

World Tour now contains eight-team knockout tournaments. Win the quarterfinal,
semifinal and final to advance a team level. A defeat or draw ends the attempt;
you keep arena XP and gold, visit the outfitter, and retry the same tournament.
Rival matches are simulated and the bracket preserves their winners and lineups.
Existing mid-tournament saves retain their current round when first migrated.

Win a final to earn a Champion's Chest. Open it from the arena report or World
Tour > Champion's vault. A chest unlocks one previously uncollected evolution,
prioritizing your headliner, then your roster. It also grants +1% permanent
health or attack until each reaches +5%. Rewards carry across local save slots
and fresh teams in trophy_vault.json; opened chest IDs prevent duplicate claims.
Unopened chests remain with the campaign that earned them. All-collection
chests award a collectible honor entry instead of increasing stats further.

Each of the 32 champions has a named ascended evolution. An unlocked champion
can awaken in the vault at level 10 after finishing pending level-up choices.
Awakening replaces its previous Guardian/Ravager/Arcanist path, keeps its four
learned abilities, and adds a fifth automatic action on a 20-second cooldown.
The tradeoff is -10% attack. Each species uses an appropriate combat effect:
Kirin lightning, Owlbear physical cyclone, Treant roots, Unicorn shields, etc.
Preview the action against clustered, lined-up or spread targets in the vault.
Ascensions reuse the established combat effects and champion models with a
larger silhouette and colored crest; they are not 32 newly sculpted meshes.

Fantasy UI: EB Garamond body text, Cinzel controls and headings, warm ivory
text, textured dark teal stone/vellum panels, brass details and softer shadows.
The font licenses ship in the Godot assets/fonts folder.

Validation: 58 tournament/legacy checks, 42 World Tour checks, 32 ascended
combat actions, UI chest/awakening/preview flow, existing ability/roster/item
regressions, and visual captures. Native Windows build checked separately.
