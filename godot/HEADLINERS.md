# Manitoria 0.20 — Headliners

## Shop with your champion's kit in view
Select a champion portrait in the outfitter. Read their actual signature and learned skills, ranks, rarity, cooldowns and evolution in the scrollable ability panel beside their equipment. Unlearned skills are not presented as owned. Higher-rank descriptions identify the base effect and the rank potency bonus; cooldowns include rank and evolution modifiers, but not conditional combat item procs.

Drag an offer onto the champion portrait or matching equipment slot to buy and equip it. The purchase saves once, and replaced gear returns to the bag. Gear can still be transferred between champions. Inspect item art for full effects and tradeoffs. The existing 48 tactical items remain intact.

## Your first signing is your headliner
New clubs choose among all 32 creatures before recruiting four teammates. The headliner costs 200g from the existing 1,200g starting fund. Tanks/bruisers, flankers and ranged starters begin in appropriate lanes. Your headliner persists in the save and appears in the club header, tournament overview and pre-match entrance.

Existing clubs retain their roster and receive their first roster hero as the initial headliner. Open any owned hero's Details and choose Make team headliner to change the face of the team. Benching does not erase that identity: the interface explicitly marks a benched headliner. The strongest active fighter is calculated separately.

## Meet the contestants
Tournament overview shows all five heroes on both sides, individual and team power, each team's strongest active fighter, and the estimated power advantage. Click any portrait to inspect abilities and equipment. Power is a roster-strength estimate, not a win guarantee or a simulation of every tactical matchup.

Meet the contestants from tournament overview, or choose Introduce contestants after arranging formation. Cyan and coral team panels slide into view; five paired reveal beats introduce the lineups, followed by a VS impact and animated headliners with creature sounds. Fight starts the actual match. Enter also starts; Escape or Formation returns to preparation. The sequence never advances combat or changes rewards. Returning to the menu saves normally.

Three original synthesized effects provide short reveal sweeps, a signing confirmation and a VS percussion/chime impact. They follow the existing effects volume and mute settings. No Nintendo audio or imagery is copied.
