MANITORIA 0.9 — HERO BUILDS & EVOLUTIONS

FOUR-ABILITY KITS
Each hero has four total slots: its original signature and three discoverable actions. Each species has eight discovery options, including its two previous abilities and six alternatives drawn from role-appropriate combat mechanics. At arena level-ups, choose from three shuffled discoveries. Offers prefer cards not shown in the previous choice while alternatives remain.

When the kit is full, cards raise the chosen abilities and signature to Rank 2 (+20% potency, 8% shorter cooldown). Unranked choices stay available until upgraded. Once every ability is Rank 2, normal levels offer vitality, attack or ability-potency mastery. Wins retain the modest rare-card advantage. Benched heroes do not earn level-up choices.

LEVEL 10 EVOLUTION
Choose once for each hero:
- Ravager: +20% attack, +15% movement, 12% basic-attack lifesteal; -10% health. Amber orbiting talons and colored spell effects.
- Guardian: +15% health, +4 percentage points armor; -10% attack. Each cast shields the most wounded ally within five arena units (including self) for 3% of maximum health. Emerald ward rings and spell effects.
- Arcanist: +20% ability potency, 15% shorter ability cooldowns; -15% attack and 15% longer basic-attack interval. Violet orbiting motes and spell effects.
These are shared build branches across all 32 species, combined with each creature's signature and chosen kit. They add gameplay and visual effects, not new sculpted character meshes.

SAVES
Existing campaigns load without a reset. Existing learned abilities and ranks are retained. Old pending rewards migrate to the new flow. Heroes already level 10 or higher receive their missed evolution once. Multiple queued level-ups generate their next cards after the previous selection, and saving/reloading preserves the current offer.

PLAY
Extract the entire Windows ZIP into a new folder. Keep Manitoria.exe beside Manitoria.pck and open the executable. Confirm Windows edition 0.9 on the menu. Existing campaign saves remain available.

VALIDATION
18 progression checks cover all 32 species, all 256 discovery entries, all 96 species/evolution combinations, progression, migration, rarity, queued rewards and combat effects. 27 regression, 39 management, 29 tactics/combat and 20 UI-flow checks passed. The evolution overlay and live arena were rendered and inspected. Evolution matchup sampling is a limited balance check, not proof of every possible team composition being balanced.
