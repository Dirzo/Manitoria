# Current model assets (3.5)

All 32 live character models are original procedural meshes. Their surfaces are generated locally in beast-materials.js; geometric details are in beast-sculpt.js. No character image atlas is loaded or shipped. The prompts below are historical art direction for retired illustrations.

# Original artwork provenance

The original three bitmap assets were generated with Codex's built-in image generation tool. Only the hero background remains in the current release; both character atlases are retired. The crest is an original code-authored SVG. The soundtrack is original procedural synthesis in score.js; no third-party recording was used.
